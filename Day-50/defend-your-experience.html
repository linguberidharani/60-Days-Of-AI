Defend Your Experience

This is a practice interviewer built for your resume. Upload your resume text, and it will pull out every claim you'd need to defend — "I built...", "I led...", "I improved..." — then run a live, adaptive screening interview based on those claims.

  • Upload your resume text (paste or drop a .txt file).
  • Review claims extracted from it before the interview starts.
  • Answer questions from an AI recruiter — each answer shapes the next question.
  • Watch your dashboard update live: confidence, evidence, communication, STAR structure.
  • Get a Defense Report at the end with your weak spots and an improvement plan.

Everything runs locally in this file. Your resume text and sessions are saved only in this browser's local storage.
