Workflow Summary

Executive Summary

A solo creator can sustain a daily cyber-hygiene YouTube channel by treating AI (Claude) as co-writer, co-editor, and co-strategist across ideation, scripting, thumbnails, SEO and LinkedIn repurposing — while keeping recording and final polish human-led for authenticity and trust, which matters most in a security-education niche.

Recommended AI Stack
  • Claude — scripting, structuring, repurposing, SEO metadata, insight generation
  • CapCut (free) — editing, captions, short-form reframing
  • Canva (free tier) — thumbnails
  • TubeBuddy/VidIQ (free tier) — keyword research
Key Deliverables
  • 1 published video/day across short/medium/long formats
  • 1 repurposed LinkedIn post per video
  • A running content calendar & topic backlog
Estimated Cost

₹0–800/month (free tools sufficient at this stage; optional paid Canva Pro or TubeBuddy Pro later).

Expected ROI

Portfolio proof for Application Security / SOC Analyst roles, inbound recruiter interest, and compounding LinkedIn authority as "CyberVidyarthi" — non-monetary ROI dominates in year one.

Learning Path
  • Week 1–2: nail the repeatable template & batching rhythm
  • Week 3–6: build a 4-video buffer to remove daily pressure
  • Month 2+: layer in basic analytics-driven topic selection
Implementation & Scaling Strategy

Start solo with heavy templating; once cadence is stable, consider outsourcing thumbnail design or editing via Fiverr, and explore Zapier/Make automation to auto-schedule LinkedIn posts on publish.
